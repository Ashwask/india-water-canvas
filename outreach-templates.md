## Outreach sequence

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Send 3 emails: 1 methodology · 2 implementation |
| 2 | Soft follow-up at day 7 if no response |
| 3 | Soft follow-up at day 14 · move on if still no |
| 3-6 | Receive · publish in `review-log.md` · iterate dashboard |
| 6-8 | Second cohort of 3 (different specialty) · same process |
| 8-12 | Y0 funding milestone preparation · build-plan.md anchor outreach |

---

## What to NOT do

- Don't email more than 3 at once initially · feedback quality drops with parallel asks
- Don't promise endorsement before review · they must trigger the badge themselves via the GitHub template
- Don't soft-launch publicly until ≥2 reviewers respond · keeps the canon open vs ossified
- Don't bypass anyone who flags defamation risk in audit lanes 1-3 (JJM · CSR · OCEMS) — that's the legal scaffolding gate
